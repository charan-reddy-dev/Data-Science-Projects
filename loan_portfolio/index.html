Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) Insights

Key empirical findings and distributions from the 40,000+ LendingClub loan records analyzed in the Capstone notebooks.

Grade vs Interest Rate & Payoff Rate

Linear interest rate escalations vs declining payoff probability

Key Finding: Interest rates climb linearly from 7.3% (Grade A) to 25.4% (Grade G). Conversely, payoff rates drop from 94.2% down to 62.1%. Grade B provides the optimal risk/reward sweet spot.

Term Duration: 36 vs 60 Months

Loan volume and relative default rates across loan tenures

Key Finding: 36-month loans comprise ~75% of total volume and have a significantly higher payoff rate (87.8%) compared to 60-month loans (74.2%).

Top Geographic Distribution

Loan origination concentration across primary states

Key Finding: California (~35.7%) and New York (~18.2%) represent over half of all loan originations. However, default rates remain consistent across all 6 key states.
